## 🧠 THE WHAT

# YOUR PHONE BECOMES THE REMOTE.

Five words. Dispatch lets you message a persistent thread from your phone, and Claude carries out the work on your desktop, using your real files, connectors, and tools, even while you're away from the machine.

### The One-Sentence Version

> Dispatch creates one ongoing conversation thread that pairs your phone with your desktop, so you can assign, check, and pick up work without being physically at your computer, as long as your desktop stays on and the app stays open.

### The Core Idea

Cowork and Claude Code both traditionally required you to be sitting at your computer. Dispatch removes that constraint for starting and monitoring work, not for where the work actually runs. You send an instruction from your phone, Claude receives it on your desktop, and executes it there with full access to your real files, connectors, and tools, then reports back to your phone when it's done or needs your input.

**The one real requirement worth knowing clearly:** your desktop app has to stay open and your computer has to stay on. Dispatch isn't magic that runs work with nothing on, it moves where you have to be, not whether the machine has to be running.

**Analogy:** think of it like calling into the office while you're out running errands, someone back at your desk is still doing the actual work, you're just not physically there to hand it to them in person. The office still has to be open. You're just not required to be standing in it.

### What Dispatch Actually Requires

| Requirement | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Desktop app stays open | The work runs on the desktop machine, not the phone |
| One-time pairing between phone and desktop | Sets up the persistent thread, roughly a minute to configure |
| A real task worth delegating remotely | Not every quick question needs this, it shines for longer or scheduled work |

### Quick Check

You want to kick off a task before leaving for the day and check on it from your phone during your commute home. What does Dispatch actually require for this to work?

<details>
<summary><strong>See a strong answer</strong></summary>

> Your desktop computer and the Claude Desktop app need to stay on and open while you're away, since the work runs there, not on your phone. Once paired, you can send the instruction from your phone before leaving, and check progress or receive the finished result during your commute, but only because the desktop machine kept running in the background.

The common misconception is that Dispatch means the work happens somewhere in the cloud with nothing running locally. It doesn't, your desktop still has to be on.

</details>

## 🔄 THE HOW

---

### "Starting a Task From the Couch Instead of Waiting Until Monday Morning"
**For anyone whose best ideas hit outside of work hours**

Someone who often thinks of a task worth automating on a weekend, far from her desk, used to just make a mental note and forget half of it by Monday. With Dispatch paired, she can message the thread from her phone the moment the idea hits, and find real progress waiting when she's back at her desktop, since the work ran there the whole time she was away.

**The idea didn't get better by waiting until Monday. It just used to have nowhere to go until then.**

*Research note: Dispatch is explicitly designed to solve exactly this gap, the requirement to be physically at a desktop for both Cowork and Claude Code, letting the phone become the place where work gets assigned and checked, while the actual execution and file access stay on the desktop machine.*

---

### "Checking Progress From the Grocery Store Line"
**For anyone juggling errands and real work in the same afternoon**

Someone running a long, multi-step task before heading out to run errands used to just accept she'd have no visibility into it until she got back. With Dispatch, she can check progress from her phone mid-errand, see what's been completed, and even redirect if something needs adjusting, without cutting her afternoon short to babysit a desktop session.

**The freedom wasn't the work itself. It was not having to choose between being at her desk and being anywhere else.**

---

### One Last Thing

Dispatch doesn't replace Cowork or Claude Code, it changes where you have to be to use them. The actual skills from those branches, delegation, multi-step tasks, building real things, still apply in full. This just removes the requirement to be sitting at your desk to start or check on them.
